    Regulatory & Research Prototype Disclaimer: RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY. Glaucoma Suspicion Score is not a calibrated probability and is not for clinical diagnosis. Model outputs are reported strictly as a "Glaucoma Suspicion Score" derived from a class-weighted binary cross-entropy objective (BCEWithLogitsLoss). Because class weighting shifts the prior log-odds, these scores are not calibrated statistical probabilities and must never be presented as confidence or probability. All screening outputs require formal diagnostic evaluation and clinical confirmation by a qualified ophthalmologist.
